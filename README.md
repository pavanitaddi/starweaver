# ✨ Starweaver

A calm, beautiful night-sky game where you don't steer anything. You bend gravity instead.

🎮 **Play now:** https://pavani123.github.io/index.html/

---

## 🌙 The Story

Lost stardust drifts across the sky, longing to reach the moon. Help it get home by weaving its path through the night.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Click **Begin**.
2. **Tap or click anywhere** to bloom a gravity flower.
3. The flower pulls the stardust toward it. Use it to bend and slingshot the stars toward the moon.
4. Avoid the **dark voids**, because they swallow stars.

## 📋 Rules

- Every star that reaches the moon = **+1 score**
- You have **3 lives**
- You lose a life if a star **drifts off-screen** or **touches a void**
- Only **3 flowers** can exist at once, and each one fades after about 4 seconds
- The game gets faster as your score grows
- Your **best score is saved** in your browser

## 🎨 Features

- Aurora sky, twinkling stars, shooting stars, and layered mountains
- Glowing star trails and particle effects
- Works on **desktop and mobile** (mouse or touch)
- No installs and no libraries, just one HTML file

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript (Canvas)

## 🚀 Run It Yourself

1. Download `index.html`
2. Double-click it to open it in your browser
3. Play! 🎉
